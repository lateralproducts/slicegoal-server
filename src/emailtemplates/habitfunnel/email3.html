During the lockdowns of 2020 in Melbourne, my habits went out the window.

My emotions were all over the place. I was hardly consistent with anything. I wasn’t sure I was being productive at all in work or my life.

When the lockdowns started, my good routine and habits disappeared. Bad habits snuck in. I was drinking too much. I was sleeping in. I was working too late. I felt exhausted. And I felt like I didn’t really know what to do.

Several times in my life, my habits have changed for the worse. When I’ve gotten sick, or I’ve gone away on holidays. Or an emergency happens. Good habits can disappear and leave you feeling frustrated. And because I wasn’t actively setting and committing to better habits, bad habits took over. And it took me ages to establish good habits again.

It has become crystal clear to me HOW important habits are. Habits create routine, create consistency, and they set you up for success. And it’s also clear to me now talking to clients, and with my own experience, that habits can disappear instantly with events and so you need to work to get them back on track.

It’s ok to fall into bad habits every now and then, we’re human! And getting back on track matters if you’re going to achieve the life you want.

6 Habits that set me up for success:
• Having a shower after work
• Going for a walk
• Waking up Early
• Gratitude Practice
• Eating healthy
• Working out
• Action the highest value thing first thing

I’ve got my good habits back now and I’m clearer in my head. I’m more productive. I’m more positive about what I can achieve, and I know what my next steps are.
